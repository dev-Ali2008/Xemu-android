# XenraOG Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 August 2026_

XenraOG processes BIOS, HDD, game images, controller input, preferences, and
diagnostic logs on your device. User-selected game and firmware files are not
uploaded by the app.

The optional cover-art feature connects to the configured GitHub cover
repository; that service can receive normal connection information such as your
IP address. XenraOG Gold activation signs you in with Patreon and sends your
Patreon account identifier and a device identifier derived from this device to
the XenraOG activation backend. The backend uses them only to confirm your
purchase and to manage your device slots; it stores your Patreon user ID and the
device identifiers bound to it. Signing in with Patreon does not give the app
access to your payment details.

Crash and session logs remain in app-private storage unless you explicitly copy
or export them. System Link can exchange a nickname, lobby information, network
addresses, and gameplay traffic with hosts or peers you choose. Passwords
entered for private lobbies are used for that connection and should not be
retained beyond the session.

Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Unbinding a device
removes its identifier from the activation backend. For privacy questions or
deletion requests concerning server-side records, use the developer contact
shown on the XenraOG Patreon page.

This is the same policy shown inside the app under Settings → Privacy policy.
