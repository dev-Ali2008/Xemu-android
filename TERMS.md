# XenraOG Terms of Service

_Last updated: 8 August 2026_

## What XenraOG is

XenraOG is an original Xbox emulator for Android. It runs software you supply.
It does not include, distribute, or provide any BIOS, firmware, or game images.

## Your content

You are responsible for the BIOS, firmware, and game images you load into

are not entitled to.

## XenraOG Gold

Gold is a paid tier purchased through Patreon. Buying it activates Gold features
on your devices.

- Activation signs you in with Patreon only to confirm your purchase and to
  manage your device slots. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
- Gold may be activated on a limited number of devices at once. You can unbind a
  device to free its slot, subject to a limit on how often unbinding is allowed,
  so that slots cannot be cycled indefinitely.
- Entitlement is checked against the Patreon campaign that sold Gold. If the
  project moves to a different Patreon account, purchases made on the previous
  account continue to be honoured.
- Refunds are handled through Patreon under Patreon's own terms.

game is guaranteed to run. Features may change or be removed between releases.
The activation service may be unavailable at times; where possible the app
continues to work on a device that is already activated.
